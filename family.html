      A FEW THINGS I’VE LEARNED ON THE ROAD

      Make room for
      the good bits.

      A little planning leaves more room
      for the unexpected stop.

      01 / PACE

      One big thing a day.

      Long drives and early starts add up. Build in a slow meal or a pool-side afternoon, especially with little travellers in tow.

      02 / THE DETAILS

      Call before you roll.

      Check opening times, entry fees, child policies, road conditions and what’s actually available at your stay. A quick call can save a long detour.

      03 / THE DRIVE

      Pack for the in-between.

      Water, snacks, sun cover and a few stops make the journey feel like part of the adventure, not just the bit between destinations.

      04 / RESPECT

      Go with local knowledge.

      Use registered local guides where needed, follow wildlife guidance and ask permission before photographing people or entering community land.
